# modAI commercial licensing

> **Commercial information only:** this document describes current commercial packaging and
> pricing. It is not a software license agreement, order form, SLA or customer contract.
> Rights to use modAI are granted only under a written commercial agreement with We Are The
> Art Makers.

modAI is proprietary software of **WATAM — We Are The Art Makers** ([wearetheartmakers.com](https://wearetheartmakers.com)).
It is delivered as an offline installation package with a licence key signed by WATAM; the source
code is not distributed. The licence key carries the licensee, plan, end date and number of users,
and the installation verifies it without going online.

Commercial licensing is available for:

- Paid pilots
- Supported production installations on the customer's own server or on the modAI Box
- Business and Enterprise deployments
- Customer-specific integrations, implementation and support

## Current commercial packages

### Pilot
EUR 2,900 one-time

A 30-day licence for up to 25 users: installation at the customer, selected company documents,
acceptance test of source-backed answers, findings and rollout recommendation. The pilot fee is
credited against the first annual licence signed within 30 days of the pilot's end.

### Business
EUR 9,900 per year

Production use by a team, business unit or company of up to 250 users on one installation, with
support and upgrades.

### Enterprise
From EUR 19,900 per year

Broader deployments: users by contract, several teams or installations, a standby machine,
custom integrations, deployment assistance and priority support.

Prices exclude taxes and hardware. In Turkey prices are invoiced in Turkish lira at the Central
Bank of the Republic of Turkey selling rate of the invoice date.

Commercial terms, deployment scope, support obligations, renewal terms, liability, warranties and
customer-specific requirements are defined in the applicable written agreement.
