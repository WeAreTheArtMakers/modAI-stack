# Customer Support Service Level Agreement (SLA)

> **FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT.** Atlasnova Yazılım A.Ş. and its product AtlasDesk Cloud are imaginary. The service levels, credits and names in this document were written only for a modAI product demonstration; they do not belong to any real organization and are not presented as legal or regulatory requirements.

- **Document ID:** CS-SLA-001
- **Version:** 3.0 (current)
- **Effective date:** 1 March 2026
- **Owner:** Customer Support

## 1. Scope

This SLA covers support for **customers** of AtlasDesk Cloud. Internal employee IT support is covered by the IT Support and Escalation Procedure (BT-PRS-005), not by this document.

## 2. Support hours and channels

| Plan | Support hours | Channels |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Weekdays 09:00–18:00 (Istanbul time) | Support portal, email |
| Premium | 24/7 for Severity 1; weekdays 08:00–20:00 for other cases | Support portal, email, phone |

Support email: support@atlasdesk.example

## 3. Severity levels and response targets

| Severity | Definition | First response (Standard) | First response (Premium) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 | Service is down for all users | 1 hour | 30 minutes |
| Severity 2 | Major feature degraded, no workaround | 4 business hours | 2 hours |
| Severity 3 | Minor issue or workaround available | 1 business day | 8 business hours |
| Severity 4 | Question or feature request | 2 business days | 1 business day |

For Severity 1 incidents the customer receives an update every **2 hours** until a workaround is in place. The workaround target for Severity 1 is **8 hours**.

## 4. Service availability and credits

AtlasDesk Cloud targets **99.9%** monthly availability.

| Monthly availability | Service credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.9% | 5% of the monthly fee |
| Below 99.5% | 10% of the monthly fee |

Credits must be requested through the support portal within 30 days after the end of the month.

## 5. Escalation

Internal escalation of customer incidents follows the Incident Escalation Guidelines (CS-PRS-004).
